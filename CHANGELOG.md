# Hollowmancy changelog

## 1.0.0 (first release)

Hollowmancy was developed under the name Arcanum. With this release it has a new name and a new mod ID,
`hollowmancy`, so worlds from Arcanum test builds will not carry their Arcanum blocks and items over.

### Progression
- Seven stages, from Mundane to Archmage.
- The Arcane Journal (Patchouli) explains everything and grows with you. You receive it when you awaken.

### Magic
- Two dozen spells across six schools: General, Elemental, Warding, Divination, Nature and Forbidden.
- Every spell grows through four tiers. At tier IV, an Archmage's six signature spells gain legendary forms.
- Spell crafting with runes and scrolls; a Spellbook to learn and prepare spells; a spell wheel and hotbar slots.
- Mana, corruption, and wands that grow with use through five levels of mastery.

### Rituals, familiars and gear
- Rituals at the Ritual Altar: infusions, fusions, bindings and the rituals that open the late game.
- Five familiars: Owl, Wisp, Golem, and from the Hollow, the Crystal Moth and the rideable Glimmerhorn.
- Arcanist's garb with fused and Hollow upgrades, artifacts, charms, holy water and arcane tools.
- Mana machines: Research Table, Infuser, Research and Verdant Engines, Mana Battery, Conduit and Beacon.

### The Hollow
- A new dimension with six biomes, each with its own music and ambience.
- Hollow creatures, ruins, towers, a sunken library, vaults, and Morvane's floating castle with its Revenant Guards.
- Bosses: the Hollow Sovereign, the Amethyst Colossus, Sir Aldric the Castellan, Morvane the Revenant King,
  the Leviathan and the Listener. Archmages can call the Echo of the Listener to fight again.
- Overworld wizard towers with four kinds of tower wizard.

### Also
- Original sounds for spell casts, the Listener, the Hollow's creatures and the Mana Battery, and music for every Hollow biome.
- An advancement tree covering stages, rituals, familiars, wand mastery, bosses and legendary spells.
- JEI and Jade support. Config options for spawn rates, landmark frequency, particles, flashing and more.
- Nearly all text is in the language file, ready for translation.
